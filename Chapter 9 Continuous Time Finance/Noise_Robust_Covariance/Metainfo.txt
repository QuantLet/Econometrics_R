Name of Quantlet: Noise_Robust_Covariance
Published in: Econometrics_R
Description: Implements common-grid two-scale realised covariance with one finite-sample factor for all entries, and bias-corrected and positive-semidefinite pre-averaging. Simulated examples verify algebraic properties and distinguish noise correction, positive semidefiniteness and invertibility.
Keywords: realised covariance, TSX, pre-averaging, positive semidefiniteness, microstructure noise, portfolio allocation, R
Author: Jiajing Sun
See also: Intraday_Fixed_Sample, Preaveraging_Covariance
