Name of Quantlet: Intraday_Fixed_Sample
Published in: Econometrics_R
Description: Reads a fixed public three-asset trade sample from highfrequency, synchronises prices on a ten-second grid and computes realised variance and the two-scale realised volatility estimator for asset AAA. Uses base R and needs no API key.
Keywords: high-frequency data, realised variance, TSRV, microstructure noise, preceding-trade interpolation, R
Author: Jiajing Sun
Datafile: sample_trades_20140917.csv, sample_trades_20140917.rds
See also: HF_Realized_Volatility_AlphaVantage, TSRV_AlphaVantage
