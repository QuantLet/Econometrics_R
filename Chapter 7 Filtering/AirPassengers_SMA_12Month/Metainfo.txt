Name of Quantlet: AirPassengers_SMA_12Month

Published in: Econometrics_R

Description: Plots monthly international airline passenger totals, in thousands, together with a 12-month simple moving average. Equal weights and sides = 2 in stats::filter() give a two-sided filter; with an even window length, it uses one more future observation than past observation. The blue line shows the monthly totals and the red dashed line follows their upward trend while smoothing seasonal variation. Observations with missing filtered values at the endpoints are omitted. The figure is saved at 7 by 3.8 inches and 300 dpi.

Keywords: Time Series, Smoothing, Simple Moving Average, SMA, AirPassengers, Seasonal Data, Trend Extraction, ggplot2, R
