Name of Quantlet: Heteroskedasticity_Tests_Corrections

Published in: Econometrics_R

Description: Illustrates heteroskedasticity tests, HC covariance estimates, weighted least squares and feasible GLS with the Engel and CPS1985 data. Robust standard errors are also reported for the transformed regressions, and variance-regression coefficients are interpreted on the log-squared-error scale.


Keywords: Econometrics, Heteroskedasticity, Breusch-Pagan test, White test, Robust Standard Errors, GLS, Feasible GLS, Goldfeld-Quandt test, Groupwise FGLS, R, lmtest, sandwich, quantreg, carData
