Name of Quantlet: AERSN_Multivariate_Inference

Published in: Econometrics_R

Description: Computes joint confidence regions and simultaneous contrast intervals for a vector time-series mean with the aersn package. Compares the affine-equivariant increment-hull adjusted-range method with Shao's quadratic self-normalization, verifies invariance under an invertible affine transformation, and illustrates joint inference on selected regression slopes. A fixed shift of the mean gives an example in which the adjusted-range test rejects and Shao's test does not. A 5,000-replication comparison reports size and power, including independent null calibration. Includes reproducible simulated inputs, figures, replication statistics and independent formula checks.

Keywords: Econometrics, Time Series, Self-Normalization, Adjusted Range, Affine Equivariance, Confidence Region, Simultaneous Inference, Brownian Bridge, aersn, R
